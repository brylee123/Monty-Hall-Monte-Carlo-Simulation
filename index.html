Why switching works: Your first pick has a 1 in 3 chance of hiding the car. If it is wrong (2 in 3 rounds), the informed host must reveal the other goat, leaving the car behind the only unopened door. The simulation uses this exact host rule. Results vary slightly each run; the lines approach 33.33% and 66.67% with enough trials.
